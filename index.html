About Ash & Moss

Made slowly, in Pokhara.

A watch should outlast the season it was bought in.

Ash & Moss started in a small room on New Road with a simple idea: make a watch in Nepal that holds its own next to anything on a wrist in Zurich or Tokyo, and price it honestly.

The name comes from what surrounds us. Ash for the grey stone and morning mist over Phewa. Moss for the green that covers the hills after the monsoon. Both are quiet, both last.

Each piece is assembled, checked and finished by hand in Pokhara, then delivered to your door anywhere in Nepal.
